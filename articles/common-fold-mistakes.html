Seven fold errors that ruin otherwise good models

Most failures do not require a new design. They require better control of repeatable geometric errors.

1. Bad reference creases

An inaccurate centerline or diagonal becomes the wrong coordinate system for every later fold.

2. Pressing before aligning

Hold a fold lightly, match the important points, then sharpen it. Do not commit geometry while it is still approximate.

3. Rounded creases

A soft bend can spring open and change geometry during handling or flight.

4. Unequal wings

Compare roots, tips and trailing edges from front and rear. Small mismatch can cause turning or rolling.

5. Twisting thick layers

Stacked nose folds can twist the centerline when squeezed unevenly.

6. Changing everything after one bad test

Do not bend tips, add nose mass and cut wings simultaneously. Change one factor, repeat, then judge.

7. Ignoring paper condition

Damp, curled or repeatedly folded sheets are not equivalent to fresh paper. Treat material condition as a variable.
