Quick rule

If you routinely get lost between two diagrams, buy clarity. If you understand diagrams but lack techniques, buy a reference. If you can fold complex models and want to create your own, buy design theory. If setup friction is the problem and you want a ready-to-fold themed activity, use the themed shelf instead.
