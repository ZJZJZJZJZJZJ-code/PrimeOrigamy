Origami paper

What paper should a beginner use for origami?

Start with accurately cut 15 cm square paper with clear front/back contrast and moderate stiffness. That scale makes reference folds easy to see without becoming unwieldy. Do not choose by GSM alone: thickness, stiffness, surface and fold memory change how a sheet behaves.

Materials

Can printer paper be used for origami?

Yes. Standard printer paper is excellent for inexpensive practice once you trim it accurately to a square. The tradeoff is that office paper can become bulky sooner in layered models, so dedicated origami paper often feels easier when the fold sequence gets dense.

Paper airplanes

Is 80 gsm or 90 gsm paper better for paper airplanes?

Neither wins automatically. We use A4 80 gsm as a baseline because it is common and easy to repeat. Moving to 90 gsm changes both mass and stiffness. Keep the plane design, launch and environment constant, then compare several throws rather than one lucky flight.

Flight diagnosis

Why does my paper airplane dive immediately?

Check symmetry first. Then inspect the nose, wing angle and trailing-edge trim. A damaged nose, unequal wings or too much downward trim can force an early dive. Make one correction and repeat several moderate launches before changing anything else.

Flight diagnosis

Why does my paper airplane keep turning left or right?

A repeatable turn usually points to asymmetry: one wing is higher, one trailing edge is trimmed differently, the body is twisted, or the launch itself is uneven. Compare both sides visually, correct the smallest mismatch, then retest.

Origami books

What origami book should a beginner buy?

Buy instruction clarity, not the biggest project count. Look for clear notation, visible intermediate states and a difficulty curve that builds one skill at a time. A technically impressive book can still be a poor first teacher if it skips transitions.

Folding tools

Do I need a bone folder for origami?

Not for normal beginner folding. Your fingers are enough for most practice. A bone folder becomes useful when consistent crease pressure or repeated sharpness is a specific problem—especially in repetitive work—not as a prerequisite for learning.

Technique

Why do my final folds stop lining up?

Late misalignment often begins several steps earlier. A slightly wrong reference crease propagates through later folds. Return to the earliest point where the model stopped matching the diagram instead of forcing the final layers into place.
