Why Prime Origami exists

Prime Origami is an independent educational site for adults, parents, teachers and adult paper-craft hobbyists. It treats folding as craft, geometry and low-cost experimentation.

Original explanation comes first. Commercial links are secondary. Every recommendation page should remain useful even if every affiliate link disappears.

Audience policy: this website is not directed to children and does not intentionally collect personal information from children.
