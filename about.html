About FoldFlight Lab

Useful before
the purchase button.

FoldFlight Lab is an independent editorial site about origami paper, folding accuracy and paper-airplane experimentation. The goal is to reduce uncertainty before a reader buys anything.

01
Original value

Decision frameworks

We explain what property matters, how to test it and what a useful next step looks like.

02
Research

Primary-source checks

Where useful, we cross-check with organizations such as NASA, Smithsonian and OrigamiUSA.

03
Commercial boundary

No copied marketplace claims

We do not manually copy Amazon prices, ratings, reviews or stock claims into editorial pages.

WHO

FoldFlight Lab Editorial Desk

The site operator selects and maintains the published material. The legal operator details are available in the Impressum.

HOW

Research, synthesis, quality checks

AI tools may assist research, drafting and consistency checks. Claims are structured around practical decisions and, where useful, checked against authoritative external sources. We do not present unperformed product tests as firsthand experience.

WHY

Help the reader make a better decision

A page should still solve a problem if every commercial link is removed. Affiliate links are a monetization layer, not the editorial premise.

Evidence standard

What we will and will not claim.

Trust comes from narrowing a claim to the evidence we actually have.

We can sayWe will not say without evidence
“This criterion helps compare paper for this use case.”“This is the best paper on Amazon.”
“NASA's activity tests design and weight distribution.”“NASA recommends this Amazon product.”
“This Amazon.de link opens a relevant category search.”“This item is cheapest / top-rated / in stock” unless current authorized data supports it.
“This experiment produced a narrow, reproducible observation” when we actually run and document it.Invented testing, fake reviews or made-up user outcomes.
Current privacy footprintThe site uses GitHub Pages, does not intentionally run its own analytics or advertising pixels, and does not embed Amazon product widgets that contact Amazon merely because a visitor opened a FoldFlight Lab page. The current setup is described in the Datenschutz page.
