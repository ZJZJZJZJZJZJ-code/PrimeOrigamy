What we do not claim

We do not say a product is “best” because it has a high marketplace rating. We do not copy prices, availability or reviews. We do not claim hands-on testing where none occurred. We do not let an affiliate destination become the evidence for its own recommendation.

Why the UX is category-first

Large-scale ecommerce usability research from Baymard consistently finds that clear category structure and subcategory navigation help users orient before browsing products. Google likewise recommends crawler-friendly site structure linking menus to categories, categories to deeper pages and important pages through direct internal links. FoldFlight Lab therefore treats origami paper, paper airplanes and origami books as distinct intent hubs instead of sending everything to one generic store page.

Why methodology is visible

Leading affiliate publishers such as RTINGS and GearLab make their testing and revenue model explicit. Their scale and hands-on resources are far beyond this site, so FoldFlight Lab does not imitate their claims. We borrow the principle that a reader should be able to tell how a conclusion was reached and where monetization begins.
