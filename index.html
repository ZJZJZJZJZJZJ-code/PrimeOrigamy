The FoldFlight method

Diagnose → test → explain → improve.

We are not trying to win by publishing the largest catalog of plane designs. The lab focuses on why a fold or flight behaves the way it does: isolate the symptom, control the variables, repeat the test, then explain what changed.

Read the method →
01

Diagnose the job or symptom

A turn, dive, stall, weak crease, layered-fold error or learning bottleneck points to different causes.

02

Change one primary variable

Hold the rest steady where practical so material, geometry, trim or launch changes can be interpreted.

03

Explain before recommending

Use the observed or source-backed criteria first; only then point to a relevant guide, book, paper or tool.
