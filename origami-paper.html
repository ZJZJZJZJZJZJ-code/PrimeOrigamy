The right paper is
model-dependent.

“What GSM is best?” is too narrow. OrigamiUSA’s paper reviews treat paper as a combination of weight, thickness, size, texture, memory, forgiveness, strength and bending resistance. Use the model to decide which properties matter.

Five-second sheet test

Make one sharp fold, reopen it, reverse it and inspect the surface. You are checking spring-back, surface cracking, thickness at the crease and whether alignment shifts under pressure. That tells you more about a specific sheet than GSM alone.
