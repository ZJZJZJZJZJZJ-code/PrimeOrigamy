## Human action required now

### 1. Gewerbeanmeldung in Freiburg

FoldFlight Lab is designed to earn affiliate commission and build a recurring commercial audience. Freiburg's official guidance defines a trade as a durable, self-employed activity carried on in one's own name and on one's own account with an intention to earn profit; actual profit is not required. The city says a standing trade must be registered at the time business begins and offers an online application.

Suggested activity description for the form:

> Betrieb eines redaktionellen Online-Angebots zu Papierflug und Origami, Erstellung digitaler Inhalte, Affiliate-Marketing sowie künftig Verkauf eigener digitaler Informationsprodukte.

Human checklist:

- Confirm the operator and business start date.
- Complete Freiburg's official online Gewerbeanmeldung (GewA 1).
- Provide the required identity evidence through the official portal; never upload it to this repository.
- Save the confirmation and fee receipt outside GitHub.
- If the authority classifies any part differently, follow its written direction.

### 2. Tax registration

Submit ELSTER's “Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung für Einzelunternehmen” to the competent tax office. Official ELSTER guidance says the questionnaire is submitted electronically, and the tax authority's current guidance states that the start of a commercial or self-employed activity must be reported through this questionnaire. Decide VAT treatment and estimated turnover/profit with a tax adviser if uncertain.

Store tax identifiers and correspondence outside GitHub.
